\subsection{Embedded computation and hardware-informed evaluation}
We additionally evaluated the implementation on a Jetson Nano running Python~3.6.9.
For this experiment, the planar model used link lengths of 0.4, 0.3, and 0.2~m,
64 motion primitives, and 40 clearance bins. An uncached analytic baseline
recomputed the same swept-risk expression online; the lookup implementation
used precomputed bounds. Both applied identical covariance, binning, threshold,
and bounded-candidate selection rules. Across 21 unique clearance/goal queries
and five shuffled, alternating-order repetitions, all 105 paired decisions
agreed. Median online computation was 0.187~ms for lookup and 172.598~ms for
recomputation; the respective 95th percentiles were 0.216 and 174.705~ms.
Offline library construction required 7.048~s and was excluded from online
timings. These measurements isolate computation and exclude perception,
communication, and actuator response; the baseline is not a competing planner.

Thirty stationary encoder snapshots informed a separate uncertainty-sensitivity
replay. After adding a stated quantization allowance, we evaluated covariance
multipliers of 1, 4, and 16, rebuilding the library for each regime. The latter
two multipliers are synthetic. Over 120 matched clearance/goal cases per
regime, adaptive-threshold acceptance was 114, 108, and 99, compared with
117, 111, and 102 for a fixed threshold of 0.05. This demonstrates policy
restrictiveness on the evaluated model inputs, not physical collision-rate
improvement. All 360 stale-input replays held. All 360 wrong-start replays
also held: 321 through the start-state check and 39 through risk infeasibility.

Finally, a paired calibration ablation used the same 20 fitting views and
20 repeat views of a fixed rigid checkerboard. Using measured encoder
positions reduced repeat fixed-target translation RMS from 14.63 to 4.68~mm,
and orientation RMS from 1.54 to 0.67~degrees, relative to using commanded
positions. These are within-apparatus consistency measurements under the
assumed 20~mm square size, not independent absolute positioning accuracy.
An unresolved RGB-pose/depth discrepancy prevents claims of calibrated
physical collision-clearance accuracy from this evaluation.
